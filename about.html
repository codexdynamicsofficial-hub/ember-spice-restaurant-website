Our Story

From a small flame to a roaring fire of flavor.

Est. 2015

Born from the Embers

What started as a small backyard BBQ passion project has turned into the city's most beloved grill house. At Ember & Spice, we believe that food is not just sustenance; it's an experience. We source the freshest local meats, marinate them in age-old Pakistani family recipes, and cook them over real wood embers.

Every dish that leaves our kitchen carries the warmth of our heritage and the spark of our innovation. We don't just serve food; we serve memories.

Meet The Chef

Chef Rashid Ahmed

With over 20 years of experience in authentic Pakistani and Mughlai cuisine, Chef Rashid is the mastermind behind our signature flavors. His philosophy is simple: "Respect the ingredient, master the fire, and cook with your soul."

- Chef Rashid
